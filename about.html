T·D TendingDaily

Built by one person, for one specific night.

TendingDaily is founded and built by Br. Lou Page, fsc — a De La Salle Christian Brother.

Why it exists

The idea started with a memorial prayer I was asked to give, and never forgot — the blank-screen night where I had eleven tabs open, half-right prayers copied from parishes I'd never heard of, trying to make something that felt made, not stitched together at midnight.

TendingDaily gives that fifteen minutes somewhere real to go: a booklet or slide deck, built around readings, prayers, and structure you choose — without needing formal liturgical training to do it well.

Where it stands

Real, working software, currently in active pilot — not a finished product with every edge sanded off. Built and maintained directly by me, no team or agency behind it. If something's off, that's useful to know, not a surprise.
