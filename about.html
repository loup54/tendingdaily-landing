T·D TendingDaily

Built by one person, for one specific night.

TendingDaily is founded and built by Br. Lou Page, fsc, a De La Salle Christian Brother.

Why it exists

The idea started with a memorial prayer I was asked to give, and never forgot. It was a blank-screen night: eleven tabs open, half-right prayers copied from parishes I'd never heard of, and me trying to make something that felt made, not stitched together at midnight.

TendingDaily gives that fifteen minutes somewhere real to go: a booklet or slide deck, built around readings, prayers, and structure you choose, without needing formal liturgical training to do it well.

Where it stands

Real, working software, currently in active pilot, not a finished product with every edge sanded off. Built and maintained directly by me, with no team or agency behind it. If something's off, that's useful to know, not a surprise.
